Protected branding includes, without limitation:

- The DevSec Blueprint;

- DSB Explorer;
- DSB certifications, badges, logos, icons, slogans, and visual identity;

- any confusingly similar name, logo, domain, account name, product name, or designation.

This policy does not claim that every listed term is federally registered. It states DSB's rules for the use of its brand assets and identifies marks in which DSB claims rights.

## Uses allowed without prior permission

You may make truthful, nominative references such as:

- "This project is compatible with The DevSec Blueprint."
- "This is an independent fork of software originally published by The DevSec Blueprint."
- "This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by The DevSec Blueprint LLC."

Any reference must be accurate, limited to what is reasonably necessary, and must not imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, approval, or official status.

## Uses requiring written permission

Prior written permission is required to:

- use a DSB logo or badge;
- name a product, service, company, course, community, repository, domain, or account using DSB branding;
- claim that a fork or derivative is an official DSB platform;
- use DSB marks in advertising, paid training, merchandise, events, sponsorships, or commercial offerings;
- issue credentials, certificates, or badges using DSB branding;
- imitate DSB's visual identity in a way likely to cause confusion; or
- register a domain name, social-media handle, or organization name containing a DSB mark.

## Forks and derivative projects

Forks must:

1. remove DSB logos and branded artwork unless written permission says otherwise;
2. use a name that is clearly distinguishable from DSB;
3. preserve legally required copyright and license notices;
4. state prominently that the fork is independent and not endorsed by The DevSec Blueprint LLC; and
5. avoid suggesting access to official DSB content, memberships, certifications, reviews, or services.

Suggested notice:

> This is an independent project based on publicly available software from The DevSec Blueprint. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The DevSec Blueprint LLC.

## Reporting misuse

Suspected brand misuse may be reported through The DevSec Blueprint's official website or published business contact channel.
